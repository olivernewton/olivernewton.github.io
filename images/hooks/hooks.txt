Buisness: Hooks Crafted Leather
Type: Videography, Photography
Video link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CKrAoJlyqGo
Perspective: We created a short promotional video for Hooks Crafted Leather to highlight the craftsmanship, detail, and character behind their handmade leather goods. Our goal was to showcase more than just the finished product — we wanted to tell the story of the brand through close-up process shots, rich textures, natural movement, and a warm visual style that reflects the quality of their work. From filming to editing, we designed the piece to help Hooks Crafted Leather connect with customers and present their products in a polished, authentic way.
