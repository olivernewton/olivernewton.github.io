name: James Honey
work: branding
About: For James Honey Company, we developed a warm, rustic brand identity that reflects their roots as a small, locally owned honey business with generations of beekeeping history. The branding focused on creating a trustworthy, handcrafted feel through a bold logo, natural honey-gold color palette, bee iconography, and supporting materials that help communicate the quality and authenticity of their raw honey. We also created printed brand collateral that highlights their story, product benefits, and connection to local beekeeping and pollination.
