Blue Bridge
2023
Branding

Blue Bridge Coffeehouse is a locally-owned café rooted in the coastal Georgia community of Kingsland —
The owner wanted a brand that felt unpretentious and genuine — somewhere between a classic American diner counter and a refined specialty coffee shop. Nothing too trendy, nothing too corporate. Just honest, warm, and deeply local.

The Honey Hole
2024
Branding, Typography
Mobile Bar

For The Honey Hole, I created a warm and playful brand identity for a mobile beer and wine bar serving weddings and private events. The project included logo design, bee-inspired supporting graphics, color palette, and a brand guide layout. The final identity gives the business a memorable, event-ready presence across both physical and digital touchpoints.

Branding, Packaging, Art Direction

Red Owl Coffee Company is a bold coffee brand identity created to feel modern, focused, and memorable. I developed the full visual system, including the logo, owl mark, color palette, typography, packaging, signage, gift card, social media graphics, and website concept. The final brand system creates a consistent experience across digital, print, and physical customer touchpoints.

Project 04

Branding

Lands & Timber
Branding, Art Direction
Land Services

For Lands & Timber Inc., I created a rugged and professional brand identity for a land clearing company. The project included logo design, color palette, typography, business cards, apparel, signage, vehicle mockups, social media graphics, and a website concept. The final system gives the company a consistent visual presence across job sites, marketing materials, and digital platforms.
