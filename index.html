Blue Bridge Coffeehouse —
Brand Identity

Blue Bridge
2023
Branding
Food Service

Blue Bridge Coffeehouse is a locally-owned café rooted in the coastal Georgia community of Kingsland —
The owner wanted a brand that felt unpretentious and genuine — somewhere between a classic American diner counter and a refined specialty coffee shop. Nothing too trendy, nothing too corporate. Just honest, warm, and deeply local.

2024
Branding, Typography
Mobile Bar

Branding

Red Owl COffee
Branding, Packaging, Art Direction
Food Service

Red Owl Coffee Company is a bold coffee brand identity created to feel modern, focused, and memorable. I developed the full visual system, including the logo, owl mark, color palette, typography, packaging, signage, gift card, social media graphics, and website concept. Branding

Lands & Timber
Branding, Art Direction
Land Services

For Lands & Timber Inc., I created a rugged and professional brand identity for a land clearing company. The project included logo design, color palette, typography, business cards, apparel, signage, vehicle mockups, social media graphics, and a website concept.
